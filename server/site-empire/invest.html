A retirement account where your money grows tax-free and you can withdraw it tax-free later.

Stocks

Ownership in a company that can grow your money over time and sometimes pay income.

Investing Glossary

Index Funds (S&P 500)

Funds that track the overall market, giving you steady, long-term growth with low effort.

Dividend Stocks

Stocks that pay you regular income (cash payouts) while still growing over time.

Traditional IRA

A retirement account that gives you a tax break now, but you pay taxes when you withdraw later.

IUL Policy

A life insurance policy with tax-advantaged growth and downside protection, used for long-term wealth strategies.

Real Estate

Property investments that generate monthly income and long-term appreciation.

Crypto

Digital assets with high risk and high reward, known for fast price movements.

Bonds / Conservative Funds

Lower-risk investments that provide stable, predictable returns and protect your money.
