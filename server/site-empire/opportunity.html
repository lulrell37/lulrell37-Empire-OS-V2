What is a 9 - 5?

A 9–5 job is a structured form of employment where you trade your time and skills for a fixed paycheck. You work set hours, follow company policies, and complete tasks assigned by managers or supervisors. In return, you typically receive stable income, benefits, and less personal financial risk. However, your earnings and schedule are usually controlled by the employer, and your income is limited to what the company decides to pay you.
