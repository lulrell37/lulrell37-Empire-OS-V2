What is entrepreneurship?

Entrepreneurship means creating and owning a system that provides a product or service to solve a problem in the market. Instead of trading time directly for money, you build something that can generate income through sales, customers, or assets. It often requires risk, long hours, and personal responsibility in the beginning, but it offers the potential for greater control over your time, income, and the direction of your work.
